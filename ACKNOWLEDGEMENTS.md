# Acknowledgements

## Sounds

The Rosewood cues in `Resources/Sounds/Rosewood/` are built from recordings in the
[Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by
Versilian Studios LLC, released under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Attribution is not required; we give it anyway, with thanks. The upright piano in VCSL
was sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio for VSCO 2 Pro; the piano is used in the
other sound sets that `tools/sounds/build_rosewood.py` renders, not in Rosewood.

The cues themselves (the WAV files in this repo) are also released under CC0 1.0.

Recordings used by Rosewood (paths inside the VCSL repository):

| Recording | Used in |

Classic, the other choice in Settings, is synthesized in the app and uses no recordings.
